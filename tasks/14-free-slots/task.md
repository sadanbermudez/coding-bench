In `calendar_tools.py` implement `free_slots(calendars, day_start, day_end, min_minutes)`. `calendars` is a list of calendars (Google, Outlook, Huawei...), each a list of `(start, end)` datetime pairs that may overlap, touch, be unsorted, or extend outside the day window. Return the free gaps inside `[day_start, day_end)` that are at least `min_minutes` long, as a sorted list of `(start, end)` pairs. Busy intervals with end <= start are ignored. A gap exactly `min_minutes` long counts.
